# TeleRO

Watch free TV channels from Romania, Europe and the Americas on Windows, in one small app. No installation, no account.

> The interface is in Romanian.

## Download

Get the latest `TeleRO-vX.Y.zip` from the [Releases](../../releases/latest) page, unzip it anywhere and double-click `TeleRO.exe`.

**Requirements:** Windows 10 or 11 with Microsoft Edge (already included in Windows) and an internet connection. [VLC](https://www.videolan.org) is optional and only used as a fallback for channels that cannot play inside the window.

**First run:** the app is not code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may show "Windows protected your PC". Click *More info*, then *Run anyway*. If you downloaded the zip from the internet, right-click the zip, open *Properties* and tick *Unblock* before unzipping.

## Features

- Romanian channels, plus news and sports channels from Europe and the Americas, with a country filter and categories.
- Plays inside the window (HLS and MPEG-TS streams); no extra player needed.
- **Favorites** tab that collects your favorites from every region, and a **Recent** tab with the last 15 channels.
- **Small window** (button or `P`): the picture in a regular window that snaps to screen edges and does not stay on top of other windows.
- Keyboard: `Up`/`Down` change channel, `M` mute, `F` or double-click full screen, `Space` pause.
- Remembers your window position and size, volume and last channel.
- Optional check that hides channels that no longer work.

## About the content

TeleRO does not host, store or redistribute any video. It shows public channel lists maintained by the community project [iptv-org](https://github.com/iptv-org/iptv) and plays the streams straight from the broadcasters. Availability depends on the broadcaster and on your country: some channels are geo-blocked or offline. All channels and logos belong to their respective owners. TeleRO is not affiliated with any broadcaster or with iptv-org.

Because it contacts many channel websites, an antivirus (for example Bitdefender) may block some of them. That does not mean your computer is infected; the blocked channel just will not play.
